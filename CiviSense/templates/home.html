How it works

From a citizen's report to a resolved issue

1. Report

Citizens describe the problem, pin the location and add a photo. The category is suggested automatically (TF-IDF + Logistic Regression).

2. Link related reports

TF-IDF cosine similarity, distance and date proximity suggest related complaints. Nothing is merged automatically.

3. Prioritise

A rule-based score shows exactly which factors drove the recommendation. Officers can override it.

4. Analyse

DBSCAN hotspots and K-Means ward clusters are mined from real historical complaint data.
